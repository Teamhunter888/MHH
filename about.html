About Mountain Hill Health

Learn about our company, our history, and our commitment.

Who We Are

Mountain Hill Health (MHH) is a pharmaceutical and healthcare company headquartered in Phoenix, Arizona.

We are dedicated to developing healthcare products and supporting communities throughout the Southwestern United States.

Our operations primarily serve Arizona, California, Texas, and New Mexico.

Our History

November 20, 2011 — Company Founded

Mountain Hill Health was established in Phoenix, Arizona, with the goal of becoming a trusted regional name in healthcare and pharmaceutical development.

2012–2015 — Regional Development

The company expanded its operations and established a presence in additional Southwestern markets.

2016–2020 — Continued Growth

Mountain Hill Health continued investing in product development, research, and regional partnerships.

2021–Present — Looking Forward

MHH continues its work toward expanding its product portfolio and serving more communities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to advance healthcare through continued research, responsible development, and a commitment to everyday well-being.
